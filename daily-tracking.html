Make tracking a habit

  1. Choose one or two activities to record.
  2. Write down the duration or completion status.
  3. Use consistent colors for similar entries.
  4. Review the week to notice patterns.
  5. Adjust your next week's plan when needed.

Use the information, not perfection

A missed workout does not cancel out earlier effort. An honest activity record can help you understand a busy schedule, recognize useful routines, and make realistic decisions about your next goal.
