Start with a plan

Set goals that fit your life.

A clear fitness goal helps you decide what to do next. Choose an action you can measure, give yourself a reasonable timeline, and use your calendar to make space for the routine.

Four steps to a realistic goal

1

Pick a meaningful target

Choose something that matters to you, such as walking regularly, improving flexibility, or attending a weekly fitness class.

2

Make it measurable

Instead of saying “exercise more,” plan to take a 20-minute walk three times per week.

3

Choose your days

Look at your school, work, and personal commitments. Choose activity days that leave room for rest and unexpected changes.

4

Review your progress

At the end of each week, consider what worked, what felt difficult, and what you would like to change.

A sample fitness goal

Target: Build a walking routine.

Action: Walk for 20 minutes.

Frequency: Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

Review: Check the calendar each Sunday.

This fictional example is specific, measurable, and flexible enough to fit around a busy week.

Celebrate milestones

Milestone idea

After completing your planned walks for two weeks, mark the achievement in purple on your calendar.

Put your goal on the calendar
